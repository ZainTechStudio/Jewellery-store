Our Story

Made slowly, on purpose.

Aurelia began in 1974 in a two-bench workshop off the Rue de la Paix. Half a century later the benches have multiplied, but the rule has not changed: one goldsmith carries a piece from wax to final polish.

We buy small parcels of stones directly, decline anything without traceable origin, and release in limited series rather than seasons. It is a slower way to build a house — it is also the only way we know to make something worth inheriting.
